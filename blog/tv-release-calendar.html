TV Buying Guide

WHEN DO NEW TVS COME OUT? THE ULTIMATE RELEASE CALENDAR

If you're waiting to buy a new television, timing is everything. Whether you want the absolute latest display tech or you're trying to time the market for a deep discount on last year's flagship, the annual release cycle can save you hundreds of dollars.

The Annual TV Release Timeline

Most major TV manufacturers operate on a strict 12-month release cycle. Here's how a typical model year unfolds.

January: The Announcements (CES)

Every year starts at the Consumer Electronics Show in Las Vegas. This is where Samsung, LG, TCL, and Hisense reveal their flagship lineups for the upcoming year — prototype technology, flagship OLEDs, high-end Mini-LED displays, and ambitious next-gen concepts.

Exception: Sony frequently skips CES for TV debuts, choosing instead to hold its own dedicated launch event later in the spring.

March – May: First Wave In Stores

The flagship models unveiled at CES begin arriving in physical stores and online retailers starting in early spring:

  • March–April: LG and Samsung usually launch their flagship OLED and premium Mini-LED models first.
  • May: Mid-range lineups and entry-level models from these brands start making their way to shelves.

June – August: Second Wave & Mid-Range Push

Summer brings releases from value-focused brands and secondary lines:

  • June–July: Brands like TCL and Hisense release their main display lineups.
  • Late Summer: Sony's premium QD-OLEDs and Mini-LEDs, along with models from Panasonic and Philips, fully roll out.

When Should You Actually Buy?

The "newest" TV isn't always the smart choice on day one. Early adopters pay top dollar (MSRP), while predecessor models sell at deep discounts.

  • Best time for discounts on previous-year models: March through May. As brand-new models arrive, retailers discount outgoing stock to clear warehouse space.
  • Best time to buy current-year models: Black Friday through Super Bowl Sunday. By late autumn, current-year models see their first significant price drops, reaching rock-bottom prices right around January/February.

If you want the absolute latest features, plan on shopping between April and July. If your goal is maximum performance per dollar, hold off until November through February to catch current-year sets at deep discount prices.
