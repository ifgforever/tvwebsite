Why size doesn't change the price

Nearly every quote you'll get in Chicago is banded by screen size — one price up to 55″, more above 65″, more again for anything bigger. It reads as fair, but it isn't really how the work goes.

A big TV is heavier and wants a second pair of hands. That's about it. What actually decides whether an install takes forty minutes or two hours is the wall: whether the studs are wood or steel, whether there's plaster and lath over them, whether it's brick that needs a hammer drill and masonry anchors. A 43″ on century-old plaster is a harder job than an 85″ on modern timber studs.

So we priced the thing that's actually consistent. One number for the install, and separate honest prices for the extras that genuinely take more time and materials.
